FAQ & policies

Good to know

The practical answers, so nothing on the day is a surprise. Every answer below is placeholder text waiting on Kassie's real policies.

How do I book?

Send an inquiry through the contact page with the treatment you want and a couple of days that suit you. You'll get a reply with availability, usually within a day. Placeholder — update if you move to online booking.

Do you take a deposit?

Placeholder. State whether a deposit is required, how much it is, and whether it comes off the service total on the day.

What happens if I need to cancel?

A minimum of 24 hours notice is required for cancellations or rescheduling. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice, as well as no call / no shows, will result in a $50 cancellation fee. This fee must be paid in full before booking a new appointment.

What if I'm running late?

Placeholder. Note how much lateness you can absorb before the appointment has to be shortened or rescheduled, since there's only one chair.

It's my first facial. What should I do beforehand?

Placeholder. Cover the useful basics — pause retinol and exfoliating acids for a few days, come with or without makeup as you prefer, and arrive a few minutes early for the consultation.

Can I book if I'm pregnant?

Placeholder. Most facials are fine; some peels and actives aren't. Say which treatments you adjust and ask clients to mention it when booking.

Do you work on teenagers?

Placeholder. State your minimum age and whether a parent or guardian needs to be present or give consent.

How often should I come back?

Placeholder. Four to six weeks is the usual maintenance rhythm for facials; acne work is closer together at first. Adjust to how you actually work.

Do you sell products?

Placeholder. Say whether you retail anything, and reassure people there's no pressure to buy — that reassurance is worth writing down.

What about photos of my skin?

Placeholder. Explain that before-and-after photos are only ever taken and shared with written permission, and that saying no changes nothing about the appointment.

Still have a question?

Ask before you book — it's easier than guessing, and there's no such thing as a silly one.
