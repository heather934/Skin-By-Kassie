What it is

Placeholder copy. Lamination resets the direction your brow hairs grow in, so gaps close up and the shape holds without daily gel.

Included

What's in the appointment

  • Brow mapping and shaping
  • Lamination and setting
  • Optional tint
  • Nourishing finish

Suitability

Book this if

  • Unruly or patchy brows
  • Anyone growing brows out from over-plucking

Keep dry for 24 hours and brush into shape each morning. Lasts six to eight weeks.
